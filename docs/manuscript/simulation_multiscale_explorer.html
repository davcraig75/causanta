CAUSANTA — how the simulation works (drag the time slider; move the magnifier; click a tumor cell)

An agent-based glioblastoma section is grown on a reaction–diffusion microenvironment. Zoom from the whole section → a tissue neighborhood → a single cell. ecDNA is the somatic instrument: random mitotic segregation sets each tumor cell's EGFR, which drives division, VEGF, migration and survival; hypoxia (O₂ < 18 mmHg) confounds them. Vessels feed O₂, VEGF sprouts new vessels, immune cells infiltrate and kill, and diffusion-limited cores go necrotic.
