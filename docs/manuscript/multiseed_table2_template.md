## Key reliability findings

**(a) Removed scenario confirms the structural causal model.** When the Hypoxia → EGFR confounding edge is set to zero (HIF=0.0), OLS and IV converge to within ~1% at both scales: 2mm OLS bias = -0.9% ± 0.4; 6mm OLS bias = -1.1% ± 0.4. This is the cleanest possible empirical confirmation that the bias documented in the baseline scenarios is driven by the modeled confounding pathway rather than by the IV machinery itself.

**(b) IV is more stable than OLS in the most-confounded cell.** At 2mm baseline (HIF=1.5, the strongest confounding), the IV β across n=5 seeds has SD = 0.162 vs OLS β SD = 0.434 — IV variance is ~2.7× lower. In less-confounded cells, IV and OLS are comparably stable, as expected when the confounding pathway contributes less to total variance.

**(c) PC discovery F1 is consistently reliable.** Across all 6 cells, F1 ranges from 0.765 to 0.916 (per-cell mean ± SD shown in the table). The single-seed F1 = 0.727 reported in the prior 2mm baseline result was a low-tail draw; the n=5 mean at that cell is 0.916 ± 0.085.
